Run a Motelwith your friends

Desert Rose Motel, Route 66, New Mexico. By day: fresh sheets and burnt coffee. By night: the escort ring in room 13, poker at the front desk, the smuggling truck, calls from the Family and an alligator named Bubba who eats whatever you throw him.

Goal: reach $1.000.000 in 7 days before the sheriff’s suspicion hits 100%.

The front

  • Front desk: check guests in before they run out of patience.
  • Dirty rooms: hold the action at the door.
  • Room 13: at night the men in hats arrive. They pay well, but the sheriff notices.

The ring (at night)

  • Lounge: at 19:00 the escorts arrive and wait on the bench next to the rooms.
  • Man in a hat: check him in and a free escort goes with him to 13 or to an empty room.
  • Trouble: if a room flashes red, hold the action at the door and throw the client out.
  • Morale: every job wears them out. Tip them in the lounge or they go home.
  • Cleaning: at the end of the day you can hire cleaners, who clean dirty rooms on their own but run from crime scenes and may tell the sheriff what they saw, or Mr. Wolf, pricey, who cleans everything, blood included, and never talks. Wages are paid at the end of the day.
  • House customer: at the lounge jukebox you can pay and go to a room with whoever is free. You’re out of play for a few seconds and come back relaxed (faster). But you risk being reported: blackmail or an anonymous call to the sheriff. The risk goes up with low morale, with the sheriff nearby and with every visit on the same day.

Other business

  • 24h Laundromat: money from the ring, the contracts, the smuggling and the poker is dirty. Hold the action at the laundromat to wash it (10% fee). Whatever is left unwashed at the end of the day makes the taxman suspicious.
  • Smuggling: from day 2, at night, a "fruit and veg" truck drops crates in the parking lot. Carry them one by one to the storeroom next to the dumpster. Crates left outside get seized by the sheriff or lost by morning.
  • Back-room poker: around 20:00 the players sit at the front desk table and the pot grows. Collect it, and hide the cards when the sheriff comes near, or he seizes the pot.

The real business

  • Phone: answer it and a mark in a grey suit with a briefcase shows up.
  • Mark’s door: hold the action. Out comes a rug with shoes.
  • Swamp or dumpster: só aí recebes. O contentor é revistado; o Bubba não fala.
  • Blood: mop in the closet.
  • Sheriff: vê num cone à frente dele (o leque azul no chão). Se vir um tapete, vai verificar; se te vir a carregá-lo, persegue-te. Foge do cone, despacha o tapete ou suborna-o. Ouve barulhos e vai ver.
  • Tasks: the list in the corner and the arrows in the world show what’s most urgent.
