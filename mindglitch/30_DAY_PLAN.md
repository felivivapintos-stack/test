## Dia 9: Brain Bug #005: The Peak-End Rule

**Clipkit:** https://www.clipkit.dev/public-editor?id=00fef225-9265-47d1-a18b-f1512906f10b  
**Texto da capa:** `WE REMEMBER THE ENDING`  
**Fontes:** Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber & Redelmeier (1993), Psychological Science

> It's the peak-end rule. We don't remember experiences by their total. We remember the peak, and the ending.  
> So end things well. Holidays, dates, even study sessions.
