## Brain Bug #001 — The Spotlight Effect

- Clipkit project: https://www.clipkit.dev/public-editor?id=75e1d6d7-b5f7-4370-902d-f38e06d75988

**Voiceover script** (model `eleven_v4`, voice Adrian Vale):

```
You walk into a room... and you're sure everyone noticed. [pause] They didn't.
[intrigued] It's called the Spotlight Effect. In a Cornell study, students wore an
embarrassing T-shirt into a room full of people. They guessed half the room would

worrying about their own spotlight. [warmly] So go wear the shirt. Follow MindGlitch
```

## Study Glitch #001 — Rereading Is a Trap

- Clipkit project: https://www.clipkit.dev/public-editor?id=75822aa4-b59a-4068-95fb-117996b0ed1c
- Length: 25 s, 1080×1920

**Voiceover script** (model `eleven_v4`, voice Mira):

```
Rereading your notes feels like studying. [pause] It's not. It's a study glitch called
the fluency illusion. The page looks familiar, so your brain goes: "I know this." But
recognizing is not remembering. In one study, students who tested themselves remembered
way more a week later than students who just reread. Same time spent. One third more
remembered. [encouraging] So close the book, and ask yourself: what do I actually
remember? That struggle is the learning. Follow MindGlitch for more study glitches.
```

**Caption:**
> Stop rereading. Start testing yourself. 📚 Study Glitch #001: the fluency illusion (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006). Free guide: The 5 Study Bugs → link in bio

**Hashtags:** `#studytips #studyhacks #studytok #exams #activerecall #learningtips`

**Cover text:** `STOP REREADING YOUR NOTES`

**Pinned comment:** `Comment "GUIDE" and I'll send you the free 5 Study Bugs PDF 📩`

## How to finish and post

1. **Voice:** in ElevenLabs, generate each script with the voice above
   (or open the flow already started: https://elevenlabs.io/app/flows/Hdkiotx7wBytLLT9CuXd).
   Download the MP3.
2. **Add the voice in Clipkit:** open the project link → drag the MP3 into *Your media*
   → drop it on the timeline at 0s. If the voice runs longer/shorter than the video,
   adjust the project *Duration* or nudge the scene groups on the timeline.
3. **Export:** click *Export* (needs a free Clipkit sign-in) → MP4 1080×1920.
4. **Post:** upload to TikTok and Reels. Add a quiet trending sound under the voice
   at ~10–15% volume. Use the caption, hashtags, cover text and pinned comment above.
5. **Safe zones:** keep nothing important in the bottom ~350 px or the right ~150 px —
   both videos already respect that.
